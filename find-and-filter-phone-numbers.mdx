Use filters and actions on the **My Numbers** page to find DID numbers, identify groups that need attention, export number data, buy additional numbers, or apply batch actions.

## Open My Numbers

<Steps>

  <Step title="Open Phone Numbers">

    In the DIDWW User Panel, open **Phone Numbers**.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Select My Numbers">

    Select **My Numbers**.

  </Step>

</Steps>

## Use quick filters

Quick filters provide access to groups of DID numbers based on status. Filters are shown only when matching numbers are available.

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="All">

    Shows the total number of DIDs available in your account.

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Active">

    Shows DIDs that are active, have remaining service time, and are configured with an inbound voice trunk and assigned capacity.

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Not Configured">

    Shows DIDs without a voice trunk or without assigned capacity. These DIDs cannot receive incoming calls until they are configured.

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Awaiting Registration">

    Shows DIDs waiting for the registration process to be completed.

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Terminated">

    Shows DIDs that have no service time left or have been canceled.

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Expiring Soon">

    Shows DIDs that will not renew automatically because the remaining number of billing cycles is set to zero.

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Porting in Progress">

    Shows DIDs that have been added to your account and are currently in the porting process.

  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

<Note>

  Manually canceled numbers with remaining service time can be restored immediately without renewal. Expired numbers are moved to the Service Aging Pool for 34 days and can be renewed during that period before being permanently removed from your account.

</Note>

## Search by number, description, country, or region

Use search filters to find DIDs by number, description, country, or region.

<Steps>

  <Step title="Open My Numbers">

    Go to **Phone Numbers > My Numbers**.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter search criteria">

    Enter a DID number or description, or select a country and region.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply the search">

    Apply the filters to update the number list.

  </Step>

</Steps>

## Use DID number filter modes

The DID number filter supports several search methods.

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="Contains">

    Searches for DID numbers that include the sequence of characters you enter. Use this mode for partial matches.

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Select">

    Lets you choose from a predefined list of DID numbers. Use this mode when you know the exact number or group of numbers you need.

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Bulk Input">

    Lets you enter multiple DID numbers at once, separated by commas. Use this mode for bulk operations.

  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Filter multiple DID numbers with Bulk Input

Use **Bulk Input** when you need to search for multiple DID numbers at once.

<Steps>

  <Step title="Open the DID number filter">

    Click the DID number filter.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Bulk Input">

    Select **Bulk Input**.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter DID numbers">

    Enter the numbers in a single line, separated by commas.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit the filter">

    Click **Submit**.

  </Step>

</Steps>

Example:

```text

1234567890,0987654321

```

<Note>

  A maximum of 250 comma-separated numbers can be included in a single search.

</Note>

<Tip>

  Bulk Input accepts numbers that include commas, plus signs, spaces, and parentheses. Other special characters are not permitted.

</Tip>

Examples:

```text

+370 (37) 123456,+52 (55) 12345678,525512345678,12746237423,12324234234

```
