---

title: "Restore and renew DIDs"

description: "Restore manually canceled DID numbers and renew DID services from the My Numbers page."

---

Use **Restore** when a manually canceled DID number still has active service time. Use **Renew service** when you want to extend service for an active or expired DID number.

## Before you begin

- At least one DID number must be assigned to your account.

- You can restore only manually canceled DID numbers.

- If a manually canceled DID number still has active service time, you can restore it without renewing the service.

- Expired DID numbers remain in the Service Aging Pool for 34 days after the expiration date.

- If a DID number was manually canceled, restore it before renewing it.

<Note>

  Use the **Terminated** quick filter in **My Numbers** to find DID numbers that were canceled or have no remaining service time.

</Note>

## Restore a terminated DID

Restore a DID when it was manually canceled but still has active service time.

<Steps>

  <Step title="Open My Numbers">

    Go to **Phone Numbers > My Numbers**.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Open Terminated numbers">

    Select the **Terminated** quick filter.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the DID">

    Locate the DID number you want to restore.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Restore">

    In the **Actions** column, open the actions menu and select **Restore**.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit the restore request">

    Confirm the restore request in the pop-up window.

  </Step>

</Steps>

## Renew a DID

Renew a DID when you want to extend its service period.

<Steps>

  <Step title="Open My Numbers">

    Go to **Phone Numbers > My Numbers**.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the DID">

    Locate the DID number you want to renew.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Renew service">

    In the **Actions** column, open the actions menu and select **Renew service**.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Select billing period">

    In the pop-up window, select the billing period.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm renewal">

    Click **Confirm**.

  </Step>

</Steps>

## Renew multiple DIDs

Use **Batch Actions** when you need to renew several DID numbers at the same time.

<Steps>

  <Step title="Open My Numbers">

    Go to **Phone Numbers > My Numbers**.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Select DIDs">

    Select the DID numbers you want to renew.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Open Batch Actions">

    Open **Batch Actions**.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Renew DIDs">

    Select **Renew DIDs**.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Review selected DIDs">

    Review the DID numbers selected for renewal.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm renewal">

    Confirm the renewal.

  </Step>

</Steps>

<Tip>

  Use filters such as **Terminated** or **Expiring Soon** to find DID numbers that may need renewal.

</Tip>

## Service Aging Pool

Expired DID numbers remain in the Service Aging Pool for 34 days after the expiration date. During this period, the DID number may be renewed before it is permanently removed from your account.

<Warning>

  After the Service Aging Pool period ends, the DID number may no longer be available for renewal.

</Warning>
